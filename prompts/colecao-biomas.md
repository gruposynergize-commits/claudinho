# CRAZY FOX — Coleção Biomas · Prompts de conceito (v2)

v2: arte em vetor de linha limpa (como a onça aprovada), sem hachura; palma sem pintas de onça,
com padrão tom sobre tom do bioma; formato da pata igual ao vetor aprovado.
Uso: anexe o vetor aprovado da onça (as 3 peças) como referência de formato e traço,
cole o MESTRE e logo abaixo o bloco do bioma. HEX aproximados das amostras.

## MESTRE

```
Premium product photograph of a pair of CRAZY FOX CrossFit hand grips floating diagonally over a background, the upper unit showing the PALM side, the lower unit showing the BACK side, with fabric wrist straps. Studio lighting, soft realistic shadows. Serious high-end sports brand.

SHAPE — follow the attached reference exactly: the grip is a tall paw silhouette, wider rounded top with soft finger bumps, a gentle waist in the middle and a straight bottom edge, with a thin darker border piping all around. Not a rectangle, not a boot shape.

MATERIAL: woven basket-weave technical fabric with a fine ribbed micro-texture. The whole piece is MONOCHROMATIC: panel, piping, artwork, pads and straps are one color family, only light to dark shades.

ART STYLE (very important): the same style as the approved jaguar artwork in the reference — CLEAN VECTOR LINE ART. Uniform thin strokes, few lines, flat, no shading, no cross-hatching, no engraving texture, no realism, no gradients. Minimal and graphic, like a logo-grade illustration. The animal is shown CROPPED and partial, entering the panel from one side (like the jaguar showing only its eyes and face between leaves), never a full detailed body.

PALM SIDE: NO jaguar spots, NO leopard pattern. Instead a subtle tone-on-tone pattern made of the BIOME PATTERN motif (defined below), drawn as simple thin line icons, scattered irregularly, low contrast. PAW PADS on top of it: four rounded toe pads and one large three-lobed central pad, cat-paw shape as in the reference, printed completely FLAT in the darkest shade — matte ink, flush with the fabric, zero thickness, no dome, no bevel, no shadow, no edge highlight.

BACK SIDE: the ANIMAL (defined below) in clean vector line art, in the darkest shade, with one or two plant elements of the biome in the same line style. Behind the animal, a minimal Brazilian flag emblem in thin geometric lines: a rhombus outline with a circle inside and one curved band across the circle — no stars, no text, no fill. The CRAZY FOX logo small, near the top finger area, rotated along the edge as in the reference.

BACKGROUND: a darker tone of the same color family with a very subtle flat line-art landscape of the biome (defined below), low contrast.
```
